The Supreme Council 33°

The sovereign power of the Ancient and Accepted Rite in England and Wales is governed by a Supreme Council consisting of nine Sovereign Grand Inspectors General. It has the responsibility for managing the affairs and promoting the well-being of the Order, including the consecration of new Chapters, and maintaining fraternal relationships with other Masonic bodies, both nationally and internationally.

The Head Office for the Supreme Council is based in Duke Street, St. James's London.

Chapters are grouped together in geographical districts (called groups in London) with authority delegated to an Inspector General.

The ceremony for Candidates takes a Master Mason to the 18°. After occupying the chair in a Rose Croix the brother may be conferred with a higher degree conducted by the Supreme Council.
